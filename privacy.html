Privacy notice

This notice describes the current DishaTara browser prototype. It is not legal advice or a substitute for a reviewed policy before commercial launch.

What happens when you calculate a chart

Your name, birth date and birth time are used only in this browser to calculate the chart. This prototype does not send them to a DishaTara server or save them in a DishaTara account.

If you tick the consent box and calculate a chart, the birthplace text you entered is sent to the Open-Meteo Geocoding API to find matching locations and their coordinates and time zone. Open-Meteo also receives ordinary connection information needed to answer the request. Review Open-Meteo's terms and privacy information.

AI questions

If the AI companion is enabled and you agree in its consent box, DishaTara sends your question, language choice, calculated chart placements and recent questions and answers in this chat to the DishaTara AI Worker, which forwards them to OpenAI to generate an answer. The site does not automatically send your name, birth date, birth time or birthplace with an AI question. The AI provider may process the information under its own terms and data-handling practices. Do not include sensitive personal information in a question.

Third-party code and hosting

The page loads the Astronomy Engine JavaScript library from jsDelivr. Your browser contacts that content delivery network to download the library, which may receive ordinary connection information. The site host may also process standard web request logs. Those providers operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

Storage and choices

DishaTara does not use cookies or local storage for birth details. If the AI companion is enabled, the browser stores only a count of successful AI questions so the prototype can show the five-question allowance; it does not store the chart or conversation there. This browser-only limit can be reset by clearing browser storage or using another browser and is not an account-level limit. You can decline the AI consent box and continue to use the local chart-based answers. Clearing or closing the page removes birth details and in-memory chat history from the page session.

Prototype limits

This is an early prototype. Do not enter sensitive information. Before a public or commercial launch, the project owner should review this notice, provider terms, hosting logs, retention practices and applicable privacy requirements with qualified counsel.
